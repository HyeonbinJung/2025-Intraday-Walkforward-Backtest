## Design and interpretation

- **Eligible candidate basket:** 100 prior-December S&P 500 member tickers, ranked by last-63-session average dollar turnover. **Not a historical market-cap top 100.**
- **Execution:** previous close signal → following tradable session open; **6 basis points transaction cost per side**, no leverage, idle cash earning 0%.
- **Risk:** max ten holdings, new position <=12% portfolio equity, 90% or 60% gross exposure cap depending on SPY/SMA200.
- **Liquidation:** -8% previous-close stop trigger, -12% peak-close trailing trigger, +20% and RSI14>72, index removal, four-calendar-month maximum holding.
- **Route-aware is post-hoc:** the distinct reversal exit was developed after studying 2024–2025 and is not independent out-of-sample validation.

This historical outcome does **not** establish that purchases followed unexplained stock declines, because the simulated code did not validate contemporaneous negative-news/filing causes or point-in-time valuation multiples.
